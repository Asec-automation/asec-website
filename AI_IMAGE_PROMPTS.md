# AI Image Prompts — ASEC Automation website

Use these prompts in any image generator (ChatGPT/DALL·E, Gemini, Midjourney, Firefly). Save each image at the **file path** shown — `.jpg`, `.webp` or `.png` with the same base name — then run `npm run build`. The site uses it automatically; no code changes.

**Shared style (already included in every prompt):** Photorealistic, professional corporate industrial photography, natural light, sharp focus, realistic detail, colour grade with deep navy and steel-blue tones and a small touch of red, no text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks.

**Honesty note:** AI images are illustrations, not photos of real ASEC projects. Use them for heroes, service and industry visuals. Keep the real photos in the *Project photos* and *Workshops* galleries, and do not put AI images on named project cards. Avoid AI images of people presented as ASEC staff where possible.
